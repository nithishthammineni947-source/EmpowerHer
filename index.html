How it works

Your text is cleaned (lowercased, stopwords removed), converted to TF-IDF vectors, and passed to two linear SVMs trained in your browser using the Pegasos algorithm: one predicts the abuse category, the other predicts severity (low, medium, high). A high-risk keyword check can raise severity, and the category then selects the matching legal sections for your region.

The training set here is a small demo set written for this prototype. A real deployment would train on a larger labelled dataset. Legal information is general guidance, not legal advice.
